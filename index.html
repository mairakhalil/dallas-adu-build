FAQs

Common Questions About
Building an ADU in Dallas

Can I build an ADU on my property in Dallas?

It depends on your property's zoning, lot conditions, setbacks, and applicable Dallas development requirements. A site review can help determine what may be feasible.

How much does it cost to build an ADU in Dallas?

ADU costs vary by size, design, site conditions, utilities, finishes, permits, and whether the project is attached, detached, or a garage conversion.

Do I need a permit to build an ADU in Dallas?

ADU projects generally require applicable building permits and plan review. Requirements depend on the property, project scope, and proposed dwelling design.

How large can an ADU be in Dallas?

Allowable ADU size can depend on zoning, lot dimensions, setbacks, building coverage, and other property-specific development standards.

Can I convert my garage into an ADU in Dallas?

A garage conversion may be possible when the structure and property meet applicable zoning, building, utility, safety, and permitting requirements.

How long does an ADU project take in Dallas?

Timelines vary based on design, permitting, site preparation, project complexity, inspections, material availability, and construction requirements.
