Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Dallas ADU Build collects, uses, and protects information when you visit our website or inquire about ADU design, permitting, and construction services in Dallas and the surrounding DFW area.

Information We Collect

We may collect personal information when you contact us, request a consultation, submit an ADU project inquiry, or complete forms on our website. This may include your name, email address, phone number, ZIP code, project type, project details, preferred contact method, and preferred contact time.

We may also collect limited technical and non-personal information such as browser type, device information, IP address, referring pages, and general website usage data. This information may help us maintain website functionality and improve the user experience.

How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to respond to inquiries, discuss ADU project requirements, coordinate consultations, communicate about requested services, and provide customer support.

Technical and usage information may be used for website analytics, performance improvement, security monitoring, and fraud prevention. We do not sell or rent personal information to third parties.

  • Information may be used to respond to ADU inquiries and coordinate requested project services.
  • Usage data may help improve website performance, navigation, and overall user experience.
  • Reasonable safeguards are used to help protect personal and website information.

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

Sharing of Information

We may share relevant information with contractors and subcontractors within our network, as well as trusted service providers that support website hosting, communications, analytics, or other business operations, when reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law, legal process, or when reasonably necessary to protect our rights, users, business interests, or security.

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to support functionality, understand website traffic, and improve user experience. Depending on your browser, you may be able to block, delete, or manage cookies through your browser settings.

Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services for hosting, analytics, communications, maps, or other website functions. These providers may process limited information according to their own privacy policies and applicable data protection requirements.

Data Retention

Personal information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries, coordinate requested services, maintain business records, resolve disputes, meet legal obligations, and protect legitimate business interests.

Your Privacy Choices

You may contact us to ask questions about personal information you have submitted through this website or to request an update or correction. Certain information may need to be retained when required for legal or legitimate business purposes.

Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our website, business practices, services, or applicable requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised last-updated date.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us at estimate@freequotepro.com or call (682) 449-8199 .
