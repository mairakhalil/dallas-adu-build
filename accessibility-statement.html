Accessibility

Learn about our efforts to make website information and ADU service resources accessible to visitors throughout Dallas and the DFW area.

  • Accessible Navigation
  • Readable Content
  • Ongoing Improvements
  • Accessibility Support

Dallas ADU Build is committed to providing a website experience that is accessible and usable for as many visitors as possible. We continue working to improve access to information about ADU design, planning, permitting, and construction services for Dallas and the surrounding DFW area.

1. Our Accessibility Commitment:

We aim to make website content, navigation, forms, and interactive features easier to understand and use for visitors with different abilities and assistive technology needs. Accessibility is considered as we review website content, functionality, structure, and overall user experience.

2. Accessibility Measures:

We take reasonable steps to support website accessibility through clear page structure, descriptive headings, readable content, meaningful links, keyboard-friendly functionality where supported, and descriptive text alternatives for relevant images and visual elements.

3. Images & Visual Content:

Meaningful website images are provided with descriptive alternative text where appropriate to help communicate their purpose or content to visitors using assistive technologies. Decorative visual elements may be excluded from assistive technology when they do not provide meaningful information.

4. Navigation & Website Structure:

We strive to maintain consistent navigation, descriptive page headings, understandable link text, and logically organized content throughout the website. These practices are intended to help visitors locate ADU service information and other website resources more efficiently.

5. Forms & Contact Features:

We work to make website forms and contact features clear and understandable by using descriptive labels, identifiable fields, and straightforward instructions. Visitors who experience difficulty submitting an online inquiry may contact us directly by phone or email for assistance.

6. Readability & Content:

Website content is written and organized with readability in mind. We aim to use clear headings, understandable language, appropriate content hierarchy, and layouts that support access to information across commonly used devices and screen sizes.

7. Third-Party Content:

Some pages may include third-party tools, maps, links, applications, or other external content that we do not directly control. Accessibility features for these third-party services may vary and are generally maintained by their respective providers.

8. Ongoing Improvements:

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. We may periodically review website pages, content, navigation, forms, images, and other features to identify opportunities for improvement as the website, technologies, and available accessibility practices continue to evolve.

9. Accessibility Assistance:

If you have difficulty accessing information, using a website feature, or submitting an ADU project inquiry, please contact us. When possible, describe the page or feature you were trying to access so we can better understand the issue and provide reasonable assistance.

10. Alternative Ways to Contact Us:

If an online feature is difficult to use, you may contact us directly to request information or discuss your Dallas-area ADU project. We will make reasonable efforts to provide relevant website information through an alternative communication method when appropriate.

11. Accessibility Feedback:

We welcome feedback about accessibility barriers encountered on this website. Feedback can help identify areas where navigation, content, forms, or other features may be improved for visitors using different devices or assistive technologies.

12. Contact Information:

For accessibility assistance or to report an accessibility issue, please contact us:
