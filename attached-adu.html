Expand Your Home With an Attached ADU

An attached ADU adds independent living space while remaining physically connected to your existing home. Our contractor network helps Dallas homeowners plan additions around the home's structure, available lot space, privacy, utilities, and a design that complements the original residence.

Considering an addition to your home? Call (682) 449-8199 to discuss your attached ADU goals.

What Matters for an Attached ADU

  • Available space beside or behind the home.
  • Structural connection to the main residence.
  • Private entrance and interior circulation.
  • Water, sewer, electrical, and HVAC needs.
  • Exterior design and roofline integration.
  • Dallas zoning and building requirements.

What Attached ADU Construction Includes

  • Site-responsive ADU floor plan development.
  • Foundation and structural addition planning.
  • Kitchen, bathroom, and living-area construction.
  • Plumbing, electrical, and HVAC coordination.
  • Permit documentation and inspection coordination.
  • Interior and exterior project completion.

Dallas Attached ADUs: A connected addition can create private space for family, guests, or flexible long-term living without requiring a separate backyard building.

FAQs

Common Questions About
Attached ADUs in Dallas

What is considered an attached ADU?

An attached ADU is an independent residential unit physically connected to the primary home, often as a side or rear addition.

Does an attached ADU need a private entrance?

Entrance requirements depend on the approved design and applicable codes, but independent access is commonly considered when planning a functional ADU.

Can I add an ADU to the side of my house?

A side addition may be possible when the property has adequate buildable space and the design satisfies applicable zoning, setback, and building requirements.

Can an attached ADU share utilities?

Utility arrangements depend on the project design and local requirements. Electrical, water, sewer, and HVAC capacity should be evaluated during planning.

Do attached ADUs require building permits?

An attached residential addition generally requires permits for structural, electrical, plumbing, mechanical, and other applicable construction work.

How long does an attached ADU project take?

The schedule varies with design complexity, permitting, structural work, utility changes, inspections, material selections, and overall construction scope.
