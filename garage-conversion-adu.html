What We Evaluate Before Conversion

  • Existing garage size and usable interior layout.
  • Foundation and structural conditions for conversion.
  • Plumbing, electrical, and utility access.
  • Windows, doors, ventilation, and natural light.
  • Property access and surrounding site conditions.

FAQs

Garage Conversion ADU
Questions Answered

Can I convert my garage into an ADU?

It may be possible if the garage and property meet applicable zoning, building, access, utility, and residential code requirements.

Do garage conversions require permits?

Permits are generally required when converting garage space into legal living space, particularly for structural, electrical, plumbing, and mechanical work.

Can an attached garage become an ADU?

An attached garage may be suitable for conversion depending on its layout, structure, utilities, access, and local requirements.

Does a garage ADU need separate utilities?

Utility needs depend on the design. A garage ADU typically requires suitable electrical, plumbing, HVAC, water, and sewer connections.

Will I need replacement parking?

Parking requirements vary by property and jurisdiction, so existing parking and applicable local rules should be reviewed during planning.

How long does a garage ADU conversion take?

Timelines depend on design, permits, garage condition, utility work, inspections, and the overall scope of the conversion.
