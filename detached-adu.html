Build a Private ADU in Your Backyard

A detached ADU creates a separate residence without altering the main home's interior. The right design starts with the lot itself—available yard space, building placement, access, privacy, utilities, and local requirements. Our contractor network coordinates these details to help Dallas homeowners plan a backyard ADU around the property.

FAQs

Detached ADU Questions
Dallas Owners Ask

Can I build a detached ADU in my backyard?

It depends on the property's zoning, lot dimensions, setbacks, site conditions, utilities, and other applicable development requirements.

How large can a detached ADU be in Dallas?

Allowable size depends on the property and applicable zoning standards, including lot coverage, setbacks, height, and other development limits.

Does a detached ADU need its own entrance?

Detached ADUs are separate structures, so the design typically includes independent access planned around privacy and practical circulation.

What utilities does a detached ADU need?

Residential plans generally account for water, sewer, electricity, heating and cooling, with connections determined by the property and design.

Do I need permits for a detached ADU?

New detached residential construction generally requires applicable permits and reviews for building, structural, electrical, plumbing, and mechanical work.

How long does a detached ADU project take?

Timing varies with design complexity, permit review, site preparation, utility work, construction scope, inspections, and property-specific conditions.
