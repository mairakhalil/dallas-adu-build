FAQs

Dallas ADU Design &
Planning Questions

How do I know what ADU fits my property?

Lot dimensions, existing structures, access, utilities, setbacks, and intended use help determine which ADU layout may work for the property.

What is included in an ADU floor plan?

Floor planning typically defines room sizes, circulation, kitchens, bathrooms, storage, entrances, windows, and other key living areas.

Should ADU planning come before permitting?

Yes. The ADU layout and supporting drawings generally need to be developed before the required permit documents can be prepared for review.

Can you design an ADU for a small lot?

Smaller properties can require more careful planning around usable area, setbacks, access, privacy, utilities, and an efficient interior layout.

Can an ADU be designed for aging parents?

Yes. Planning can prioritize convenient circulation, accessible entries, practical bathroom layouts, and other features that support long-term family use.

How long does ADU design and planning take?

Timing varies with property complexity, ADU type, revisions, required drawings, site information, and the scope of permit preparation.
